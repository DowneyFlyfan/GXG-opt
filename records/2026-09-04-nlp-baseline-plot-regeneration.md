# Historical NLP plot regeneration after AdamW/Muon retune

## Scope

The retained `gpt_12x512` WikiText task has new canonical AdamW and Muon
metrics, both at learning rate `6e-4`.  Historical candidate experiments on
that *same task* previously embedded the older baseline curves.  Their
candidate metric JSON Lines files were not changed; only their comparison
figures were regenerated.

The refresh covered 122 tracked PNG files plus the two untracked continuation
plots for the active spectral-sphere run.  It includes Hybrid Stiefel-Muon,
Stiefel-Muon, spectral-sphere Muon, Recycled Kronecker--Riemannian
preconditioned conjugate gradient, low-rank secant, and the aggregate
Gauss--Newton plot.  Each eligible chart now uses the canonical files:

- `metrics/nlp/nlp_gpt_12x512__adamw.jsonl`
- `metrics/nlp/nlp_gpt_12x512__muon.jsonl`

The baseline curves carry their measured five-epoch wall-clock durations:
AdamW 10,030.85 seconds and Muon 10,416.55 seconds.  As validation is measured
at epoch boundaries, those baseline points use their epoch fraction of the
measured total duration for Metric--Time figures.

## Method

`src/regenerate_nlp_baseline_plots.py` discovers existing GPT candidate plots,
loads the immutable candidate trace, synthesizes the canonical baseline's
step/time coordinates, and writes the corresponding Metric--Steps and
Metric--Time PNG files.  The dedicated Stiefel and Hybrid Stiefel plot writers
then redraw their own series so their established labels remain intact.

CV (Computer Vision), Audio, and SmolLM2 figures were intentionally left
unchanged: their datasets/models differ from `gpt_12x512`, so injecting this
language-model baseline would be an invalid comparison.
